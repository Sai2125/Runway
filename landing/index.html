The problem

ADHD isn't a memory problem. It's a now / not-now problem.

Anything not in this moment effectively doesn't exist. We know about the birthday, the deadline, the "I'll send it tonight." It's just not in our head at the moment it becomes actionable. Every productivity tool fails us in the same four ways:

01
the day

It reminds you on the day.

Zero runway. A birthday alert at 9am is a guilt notification, not a plan.

02

It shows you everything.

The backlog becomes a wall. So you stop opening the app. Three weeks, tops.

03

It trusts your estimate.

You said 30 minutes. You always say 30 minutes. It was never 30 minutes.

04

It goes red when you miss.

Overdue. Overdue. Overdue. The shame spiral is the real reason the app gets deleted.

Principle one

The first memory tool whose main feature is not showing you things.

Dump twelve things in. The screen shows one. Everything else waits below the horizon — out of sight, on purpose — until there's still time to act on it.

How it works

Talk to it like a friend. It handles the time part.

No forms. No fields. No onboarding. Type it, say it, or send a photo of the scribble on your hand.

01

Dump

Text, voice, a photo of handwriting. One message can hold five commitments.

"I'll send Rahul the deck tonight, and Priya's bday is on the 20th."
02

Hide

It understands what, when, who — then puts it below the horizon. You see nothing.

"Got both. Go do your thing."
03

Surface

One thing comes back when there's still runway, with a concrete first step.

"Priya's Saturday. Gifts take you 4 days to actually order. Pick one now? Three ideas →"
04

Follow up

Checks in like a nosy friend. Backs off when told. Never a cop.

"You told Rahul 'tonight' at 4:12. It's 9. Tonight is getting thin."
05

Slip gracefully

Nothing goes red. It drafts the message. One tap, loop closed.

"That one got away. Tell Rahul? — 'Deck's coming tomorrow AM, sorry for the slip.'"
06

Learn

Every said-vs-actual feeds your personal time multiplier. It plans with the real you.

"You said 30. History says 2.4×. Blocking 75."
